- Install `sale_timesheet` (Timesheets with Sales); the module is installed
  automatically together with it and the Papierkram import.
- The goods and services of Papierkram become products with the VAT rate of
  Papierkram (none for a small business, the option _Small Business_ of the
  import). Services used in time entries are invoiced by timesheets. The income
  account comes from the product category: check it after the import.
- Persons of the time entries without a user in Odoo get an employee of the
  same name in the company of the import; link them to users later if needed.
