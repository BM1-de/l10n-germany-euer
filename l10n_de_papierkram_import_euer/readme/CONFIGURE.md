Nothing to configure in the module itself. Before the import:

- Enable the EÜR for the company (_Settings → Accounting_), otherwise the
  depreciation is posted as journal entries as in Papierkram.
- Check the VAT status periods of the business (_Configuration → EÜR → VAT
  Status Periods_). Without any, the import creates one from the first
  exported year with the treatment chosen in the import (small business or
  standard taxation).
- Decide the _Takeover Date (Asset Register)_: normally 31 December of the last
  year computed and filed with Papierkram.
