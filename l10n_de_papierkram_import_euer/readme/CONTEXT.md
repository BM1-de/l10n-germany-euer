The asset register of `l10n_de_euer` is the only source of the depreciation of
the cash-basis profit computation: depreciation posted as journal entries is
never counted, so nothing counts twice. Papierkram, however, posts its
depreciation itself. This bridge decides at a takeover date which side
depreciates: Papierkram up to the last year filed with it, the register from
then on. The years filed with Papierkram stay as filed.
