Takes over the fixed assets of a Papierkram import into the asset
register of the cash-basis profit computation (``l10n_de_euer``).
Installed automatically together with both modules.

With the register, the depreciation of the EÜR comes only from the
register; depreciation posted as journal entries is not counted. The
import therefore splits the assets at a takeover date, the 31 December
of the last year computed in Papierkram:

- Assets acquired up to the takeover date are taken over with the
  register import of ``l10n_de_euer``: cost, useful life and the
  Papierkram book value at the takeover date, including Papierkram's
  memo value of 1 €. Their depreciation is posted as journal entries
  only up to the takeover date, as history; the years up to then stay as
  filed with Papierkram.
- Assets acquired later are entered in the register as purchases, linked
  to the journal items of their voucher. The register depreciates them
  in full.

Without a VAT treatment of the business, the import enters the treatment
of the import (small business or standard taxation) from the first
exported year on.

Use Cases / Context
===================

The asset register of ``l10n_de_euer`` is the only source of the
depreciation of the cash-basis profit computation: depreciation posted
as journal entries is never counted, so nothing counts twice.
Papierkram, however, posts its depreciation itself. This bridge decides
at a takeover date which side depreciates: Papierkram up to the last
year filed with it, the register from then on. The years filed with
Papierkram stay as filed.

Configuration
=============

Nothing to configure in the module itself. Before the import:

- Enable the EÜR for the company (*Settings → Accounting*), otherwise
  the depreciation is posted as journal entries as in Papierkram.
- Check the VAT status periods of the business (*Configuration → EÜR →
  VAT Status Periods*). Without any, the import creates one from the
  first exported year with the treatment chosen in the import (small
  business or standard taxation).
- Decide the *Takeover Date (Asset Register)*: normally 31 December of
  the last year computed and filed with Papierkram.

Usage
=====

1. Enable the EÜR for the company (*Settings → Accounting*) before the
   import.
2. In the Papierkram import, *Take Over Fixed Assets (EÜR)* is then
   switched on. Set the *Takeover Date (Asset Register)* to 31 December
   of the last year computed (and filed) with Papierkram. To let the
   register compute the whole history instead, choose the 31 December
   before the first acquisition.
3. Check and run the import as usual. The log lists the assets taken
   over and anything to enter by hand; *Assets* opens them in the
   register.

If a row cannot be taken over (e.g. an asset pool whose book value does
not follow one fifth per year), nothing is taken over; the log shows the
rows. Correct them and take the assets over with *Asset Register → Take
Over Asset Register*.

An import made before the EÜR was enabled keeps its depreciation
entries; the button *Take Over Fixed Assets (EÜR)* on the import takes
its assets over afterwards with the takeover date set there.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- The takeover date is always a 31 December. The years up to it stay as
  filed with Papierkram; the register does not compute them again.
- Assets taken over keep the memo value of 1 € that Papierkram applies
  (warning C-63 of ``l10n_de_euer``); assets entered in the register as
  purchases are depreciated in full.
- An asset whose voucher or asset category is missing is not taken over
  and has to be entered in the register by hand; the log lists it.
- The register import checks all rows together: one row that cannot be
  taken over (e.g. an asset pool whose book value does not follow one
  fifth per year) stops the takeover of all assets acquired up to the
  takeover date.
