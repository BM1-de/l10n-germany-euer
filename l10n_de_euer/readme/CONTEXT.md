A sole proprietor who determines the profit under § 4 (3) EStG has to file the
form *Anlage EÜR* electronically every year, together with *Anlage AVEÜR* for the
fixed assets. Unlike a balance sheet, the cash-basis method follows the payments:
an invoice counts when it is paid, not when it is issued or booked.

Odoo's standard reports and `l10n_de_mis_reports` work with the posting date of
the journal entries. This module instead follows every real money movement through
the reconciliation graph to the original document, keeps the forms of every year
as data and produces a fill-in aid for *Mein ELSTER*. It never transmits data
itself.
