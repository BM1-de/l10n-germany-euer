The German cash-basis profit statement (EÜR) in Odoo

Anlage EÜR, AVEÜR and SZ computed from the payments in Odoo, with a fill-in aid for Mein ELSTER

What it does

  • Inflow and outflow (§ 11 EStG) straight from the bank and cash lines in Odoo; every amount can be traced down to its document.
  • The official forms 2024–2026 (Anlage EÜR, AVEÜR, SZ) as data: lines, codes, official wording, totals and transfers.
  • Small business and regular VAT, private shares, gifts, entertainment, VAT payments and the 10-day rule with a checklist for the turn of the year.
  • Asset register with straight-line and declining-balance depreciation, low-value assets, asset pools and the Anlage AVEÜR.
  • Checks before finalising, finalisation with a checksum, corrections.
  • Fill-in aid for Mein ELSTER (HTML and PDF), evidence per code down to the document, registers according to § 4 (7) EStG, codes as CSV and JSON, data package Z3 (§ 147 (6) AO) and an audit log.

How it works

  1. Install it in a company with the German chart of accounts SKR03 or SKR04 (l10n_de).
  2. Invoicing → Configuration → EÜR → EÜR Setup: enable the EÜR, enter the type of business, legal form and type of income, and choose small business or regular VAT.
  3. Invoicing → Reporting → EÜR → Check Account Assignment shows which account goes into which line; confirm or change uncertain ones.
  4. Book as usual: invoices, vouchers and bank statements.
  5. Invoicing → Reporting → EÜR → Statements → New, choose the year and click Compute.
  6. Answer the questionnaire, classify open rests, decide the checklist for the turn of the year, enter the bank balance of 31 December and click Run Checks.
  7. Have the form year released by a qualified person who did not create the data, then Finalise and open the Fill-in Aid.

Good to know

  • Alpha, not independently checked: the calculation, the reference cases and the form data have been created and checked by the maintainer only; a review by a tax professional is still pending. The form years ship as drafts, and a statement can only be finalised after a qualified person has released them.
