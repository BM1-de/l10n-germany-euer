Germany EÜR

This module computes the German cash-basis profit statement (Einnahmenüberschussrechnung, § 4 (3) EStG) with the official forms Anlage EÜR and Anlage AVEÜR. Income and expenses count in the year the money comes in or goes out (§ 11 EStG), traced from the bank and cash lines in Odoo back to the original document.

The module provides:

  • the official forms 2024, 2025 and 2026 (Anlage EÜR, AVEÜR and SZ) as data, every line with a stable code FORM.BOX.KZ (e.g. EUER.20.111), its line number per year, the official wording, sign rules and formulas for all sums and transfers, and the rule parameters per year with their legal source;
  • the EÜR categories with their form fields per year and the default account assignment for SKR03 and SKR04 (with proposals for accounts you create);
  • the computation of a year: every receipt and payment is traced over the reconciliations to its document and split into slices with cash date, year and form field (§ 11 EStG, ten-day rule, VAT of small businesses and standard taxation, private withdrawals and contributions, gifts and entertainment);
  • the asset register with depreciation, low-value assets, asset pools and Anlage AVEÜR;
  • the statement of the year with the questionnaire on the program scope, profit corrections with own receipts, the bank reconciliation of 31 December, more than 60 checks, the finalisation with a SHA-256 hash chain and corrections as new versions;
  • the outputs: an accessible fill-in aid for Mein ELSTER (HTML and PDF), the evidence per Kennzahl down to the receipt, a workbook with the registers of § 4 (7) EStG, the list of the Kennzahlen as CSV, a machine readable data set (JSON), the data access package Z3 with index.xml (§ 147 (6) AO), the check protocol and a template of the procedure documentation;
  • the setup of a business and the takeover of open items from a previous system.

Important notice: This module is a tool for preparing the cash-basis profit statement according to § 4 (3) EStG and the form Anlage EÜR. It does not replace tax advice. The taxpayer alone is responsible for the correctness and completeness of the tax return and of the data transmitted to the tax office. Check all values before transmission, in particular for new form years. The module does not transmit any data to the tax authorities and does not change any journal entries. The program scope and the cases that are not supported are listed in the section “Known issues / Roadmap”.

The software is provided without any warranty (sections 15 and 16 of the GNU AGPL-3.0). Mandatory statutory liability remains unaffected.

Development status Alpha, not independently checked: the calculation, the two reference cases and the data of the form years were created and checked by the maintainer only; an independent check by a tax expert is still pending. The form years are delivered as drafts: you can compute and compare a statement, but you can only finalize it after a qualified person who did not create the data has released the form year (see “Configuration”).

Use Cases / Context

A sole proprietor who determines the profit under § 4 (3) EStG has to file the form Anlage EÜR electronically every year, together with Anlage AVEÜR for the fixed assets. Unlike a balance sheet, the cash-basis method follows the payments: an invoice counts when it is paid, not when it is issued or booked.

Odoo’s standard reports and l10n_de_mis_reports work with the posting date of the journal entries. This module instead follows every real money movement through the reconciliation graph to the original document, keeps the forms of every year as data and produces a fill-in aid for Mein ELSTER. It never transmits data itself.

Configuration

Setup of a business (Accounting, Community: Invoicing, → Configuration → EÜR → EÜR Setup, accounting managers). The wizard collects the settings in one place; it books nothing and never sets a lock date.

  1. Enable the cash-basis profit statement for the company and enter the header values of the form: type of business, legal form, type of income and owner.
  2. VAT Status Periods: small business (§ 19 UStG) or standard taxation, with the return period and the permanent extension (Dauerfristverlängerung). The treatment is never derived from the taxes of the bookings.
  3. Small businesses switch the default accounts and taxes to the accounts without VAT (SKR03 8195/3200, SKR04 4185/5200); otherwise every booking with VAT is reported (C-30).
  4. Private matters: the private share account of the purchase journals takes the private share of a bill (e.g. of a phone bill); the private accounts (SKR03 1800/1890, SKR04 2100/2180) give the withdrawals and contributions (Kz 122/123). Mark the money journal of a private account without a separate business account as private account (R-18): business income received there is a withdrawal, business expenses paid from it are a contribution.
  5. Payment providers and credit cards (PayPal, Stripe, card accounts): the booking decides when the money counts. With a bank journal of its own the money counts when it is credited to the provider or card account; booked on a reconcilable clearing account it counts with the payout to the bank account.
  6. Business entertainment: by default 70 % are booked on 4650 (SKR04 6640) and 30 % on 4654 (6644). If you book the full amount on 4650, choose Split 70/30 automatically (also in Accounting → Configuration → Settings → EÜR).
  7. Lock date: set the global lock date (fiscal year lock date) to 31 December of a year before its statement is finalised. Prefer it to the hard lock date, which allows no exception for December lines imported late (C-70).
  8. Takeover from a Previous System: the open receivables and payables of the opening entries get their category, VAT treatment and rate (C-35); the turnover of the previous year goes into the chosen VAT status period, whose current input the dialog shows (an empty amount stores nothing, tick No Turnover in the Previous Year to confirm 0); the asset register of the previous system is imported by CSV (Take Over Asset Register).

Then check the account assignment of the business (Check Account Assignment, see Usage).

Form data. No configuration is needed for the form data.

  • Form years, form fields and rule parameters are shared by all companies. They are listed under Accounting (Community: Invoicing) → Configuration → EÜR and can only be changed by system administrators.
  • A form year is delivered in state Draft. It is set to Verified only after the review by a person who did not create the data (tax advisor or second maintainer); name, role and date of the review are mandatory.
  • Corrections of a delivered form year are added as a new version with its own records. The old version is marked as superseded and stays unchanged as soon as a finalised statement uses it.
  • The form years are delivered with the module; you need no generator. The maintainers generate a new form year from the BMF letter with the official forms and instructions (PDF published by the Federal Ministry of Finance) and a table of its lines, Kennzahlen, labels and formulas prepared from it; that table and the notes are not part of the repository. The generator _dev/werkzeuge/gen_forms.py (option --spec, folder of the table) checks every pair of line and Kennzahl, all sums and transfers and writes data/forms/euer_<year>.xml; _dev/werkzeuge/check_forms.py then compares the delivered data with the text of the official PDF (pdftotext) for the release of the form year.

Account assignment. Every account gets an EÜR category per business (main company with its branches) and period. The category says how the account is treated (money, open item, tax, income, expense, private, fixed asset, depreciation, opening balance, neutral) and, per form year, into which fields its amounts go.

  • Companies with the chart template SKR03 or SKR04 get the default assignment of all template accounts when the module is installed, and new companies when their chart template is loaded. Bank, cash and credit card accounts, suspense and transfer accounts are assigned from the journals. Only missing assignments are created; corrections are never overwritten, also not by reloading the chart template.
  • Accounts you create yourself get a proposal (from the code range of the chart template), never a silent assignment. Other charts of accounts have no defaults; the checks list every account that is missing.
  • Assignments with low confidence (ambiguous cases A01–A34) must be confirmed once the account is used. A change of use gets a new period (New Period); a correction of an error changes the record. All changes are logged.
  • Start of the Activity (tab EÜR of the main company, EÜR Setup): in the year the activity starts the small business limit of the current year is 25,000 € instead of 100,000 € and there is no previous year (§ 19 (1) UStG from 2025, section 19.1 (3) UStAE). A turnover of the previous year entered on a VAT status period (takeover from another system) counts for the year entered next to it (For the Year, empty: the first year of the period) only; later years take the turnover from the data. A turnover of 0 counts only with its year (in the takeover: No Turnover in the Previous Year).
  • Gifts: the category Gifts (deductible) allows collective entries without recipient for low-value promotional gifts (R 4.11 (2) EStR), booked on the gift accounts 4630 (SKR04 6610). The DATEV account Streuartikel 4605 (6605) stays an advertising account (Kz 224) and is not part of the gift check.
  • Gifts per recipient: enter the recipient on the bill or the bank entry (tab EÜR, Recipient of the Gifts). The partner of a bill or a bank entry is the shop, never the recipient; without a recipient a gift is blocked (C-51). A bill with gifts for several recipients gets the recipients per line (All EÜR Information → Recipients of the Gifts per Line, with the number or share of the gifts of the line). The 50 € limit counts all gifts of the year to the recipient, paid or not, by the date the gift is handed over: enter Date of the Gift if it differs from the date of supply (bought in December, handed over in January). Gifts booked on the account for non-deductible gifts (4635, SKR04 6620) stay non-deductible without recipient.
  • SKR03 4980: in DATEV it is Sonstiger Betriebsbedarf (work equipment, Kz 229, like SKR04 6850). The German name of the Odoo template wrongly says Mietleasing … technische Anlagen; the assignment must therefore be confirmed once the account is used.
  • Categories and their fields per form year are delivered as data and can only be changed by system administrators. The maintainers generate the defaults with _dev/werkzeuge/gen_mapping.py from a classification of every account of the SKR03 and SKR04 templates of Odoo (not part of the repository; option --spec) and the template files of l10n_de (option --templates).

Usage

Course of the year. Book as usual during the year; the module only reads the bookings. For the statement of a year:

  1. Import the bank and cash statements up to 31 December, including the last December lines, before you set a lock date (Odoo moves the date of an entry in a locked period).
  2. Create the statement of the business and year (Accounting → Reporting → EÜR → Statements, Community: Invoicing → Reporting → EÜR) and answer the Questionnaire; an accounting manager confirms it.
  3. Compute the statement.
  4. Classification of Rests: classify every payment the computation cannot assign to a document (advance payment, refund, write-off, returned direct debit; C-20, C-36, C-37).
  5. Turn of the Year: decide every item of the review list with a reason: regularly recurring payments within ten days around 31 December, such as rent or VAT returns (C-26), and transfers ordered in December but debited in the first days of January (C-23); then compute again. Apply needs the economic year the payment belongs to (the year before for a payment in January, the year after for one in December): with the year of payment the decision moves nothing and is reported (C-26). For a direct debit of the tax office whose due date falls on a weekend (10 January 2026 and 2027) the module proposes the year of payment: it reads the period for the payment as ending on the next working day (§ 108 (3), § 224 (2) no. 3 AO, H 11 EStH). Apply stays possible, but raises a warning (C-26) that must be acknowledged with a reason before the finalisation. The legal question is open (see Known issues / Roadmap).
  6. Bank Reconciliation 31 December: enter the balance per statement of account of every money journal and attach the statement (C-38).
  7. Profit Corrections: enter the values without bookings (private use, flat rates, home office) with a reason and an own receipt.
  8. Run Checks: solve the blockers and acknowledge the warnings with a reason.
  9. Set the global lock date (fiscal year lock date) to 31 December and Finalise (accounting managers).
  10. Enter the values in Mein ELSTER with the Fill-In Aid and keep the outputs with the receipts.
  11. Enter the date and the transfer ticket of the submission on the tab Finalisation.

Booking rules. The computation follows the money through the reconciliations. Book so that every payment reaches its document:

  • Book a returned direct debit as a statement line of its own and reconcile it with the receivable account of the customer: it creates a new open item. Do not undo the reconciliation of the original payment. Reconcile the next payment of the customer with this new item and book the fee on the costs of money transfer (SKR03 4970, SKR04 6855). Write off an amount the customer never pays only through the classification (C-36).
  • Wages, advance payments received and other amounts booked on a balance account that counts at payment (payroll liabilities SKR03 1740–1742, advance payments 1710–1720 and the like) count when they are paid: the entry “4120 to 1740” is only documented (C-03), the payment “1740 to bank” is the expense. Non-cash benefits booked against revenue (e.g. “4120 to 8590”) count on the date of the entry.
  • Do not book bills against a liability account that cannot be reconciled (e.g. “4930/1576 to 1700” and later “1700 to bank”): such an expense would get lost (blocker C-18, C-04). Enter the bill as a vendor bill on the payable account of the partner, or make the account reconcilable.
  • Transfers between tax accounts (e.g. the VAT settlement “1776 to 1789”) and between balance accounts (e.g. a loan “1700 to 0630”) count nothing: VAT counts when it is paid to or refunded by the tax office (Kz 186, 141).
  • Reclassify an expense in the year it was paid: a transfer between expense accounts counts on its date (C-03 points out a different year).
  • Do not book depreciation in the ledger: the asset register is the only source of depreciation, low-value assets and asset pools (C-68 compares the ledger with it).
  • Enter the recipient of gifts on the bill or the bank entry (C-51), and book a private share of a purchase with the private share account of the purchase journal (EÜR Setup).

Form data and account assignment (Accounting, Community: Invoicing, → Configuration → EÜR):

  1. Form Years shows the official forms per assessment year with their source (BMF letter, form ids, checksum of the evaluated PDF).
  2. Open Form Fields to look up a line: the stable code, the Kennzahl, the line of the respective year, the official wording, the formula and the program scope (supported, manual input or not supported).
  3. Rule Parameters lists the amounts and rates of the tax rules with their period of validity and legal source.
  4. Account Assignment lists the EÜR category of every account with period, confidence and confirmation; the account form shows the current category.
  5. Check Account Assignment: choose the business and year. Show Accounts lists every account with posted lines in the year with category, confidence, confirmation, proposal and amount; filter for accounts that are not assigned, with low confidence and unconfirmed, or not provided for in the EÜR. Apply the proposal or confirm the assignment from the list. Run Checks stores the checks C-01 to C-09, C-30, C-31 and C-50 under EÜR Checks; warnings are acknowledged with a reason.

Asset register (Accounting → Asset Register (EÜR)): the register is the only source of depreciation, low-value assets, asset pools and Anlage AVEÜR; depreciation booked in the ledger is compared with it, never counted twice.

  1. Create an asset from the journal items of a purchase (list of journal items, action Create EÜR Asset): delivery date, net cost, input tax and AVEÜR group are proposed. Check the kind (depreciable, low-value asset, asset pool …), the method and the useful life, then Enter in Register.
  2. Additional costs, subsidies, special depreciation (§ 7g (5) and § 7b EStG), extraordinary depreciation and changes of the useful life or the business share are events of the asset. Yearly Values shows the calculation per year; a use withdrawal for private use is confirmed or rejected there. A building with linear depreciation keeps its percentage (§ 7 (4) sentence 1 EStG): later costs, subsidies and extraordinary depreciation change its basis; enter a remaining useful life with such an event only to spread the book value over it. A contributed asset is depreciated over its remaining useful life, which is proposed from the private acquisition.
  3. A sale, withdrawal or scrapping is entered on the asset and recorded with Record Disposal.
  4. Take Over Asset Register (accounting managers) imports the register of a previous system by CSV with the book values at the takeover date; the years before are not calculated again. The preview tries every row and shows all errors before anything is imported. Special depreciation under § 7g EStG of the previous system belongs in the columns Sonder-AfA § 7g and Jahr Sonder-AfA § 7g: after its period the register spreads the book value over the remaining useful life (§ 7a (9) EStG); the preview points out book values below the linear depreciation. Enter the book values per group of the filed Anlage AVEÜR to have them checked in the following year.
  5. Check Asset Register runs the checks C-30, C-40, C-42 and C-61 to C-69. Compute Year runs them as well, with the computed form values, so C-62 compares the transfers into Anlage EÜR as computed.

Computation of the year (Accounting → Reporting → EÜR → Compute Year, Community: Invoicing → Reporting → EÜR): choose the business and the year. The computation reads the bookings, assigns every inflow and outflow to its year (§ 11 EStG) and stores the result under EÜR Calculations with its slices and checks. The form values combine the payments with the amounts of the asset register of the same business (depreciation, low-value assets, asset pools, withdrawals and contributions of assets): the purchase of an asset is neutral, and depreciation booked in the ledger is not counted. A branch marked as separate business has its own computation and its own asset register.

Statement of the year in detail (Accounting → Reporting → EÜR → Statements, Community: Invoicing → Reporting → EÜR): one statement per business and year.

  1. Questionnaire: answer the questions on the program scope; an accounting manager confirms them (Confirm Questionnaire). The answers of the previous year are proposed, but count only after the confirmation. A case outside the program scope blocks the finalisation.
  2. Profit Corrections: values without bookings, i.e. private use of vehicles (Kz 106), withdrawals of goods, use or services (Kz 108) and the VAT on them, business trips with a private vehicle (Kz 147), commuting allowance (Kz 176 and 142), daily home office allowance (Kz 163), home office (Kz 172 and 162), additional meal expenses (Kz 171), flat rate of business expenses (Kz 195), tax-free income and § 3c expenses (Kz 240 to 245), income in kind and barter (Kz 111, 112 or 103). The amounts are computed with the rule parameters of the year; the yearly maximums hold for all corrections of the year together; withdrawals and contributions (Kz 122 and 123) follow automatically. A small business enters no VAT; a taxable free supply (input tax deducted on the good or its costs) needs its VAT base, which counts in the total turnover of the small business limit. Every correction needs a reason; attach the own receipt. Use withdrawals proposed by the asset register can be taken over as corrections; the register then leaves them out.
  3. Compute runs the computation of the year with the corrections and fills Anlage EÜR and AVEÜR with line, Kz, previous year and source. From a value, Show records leads to its slices. A value can be overridden with a reason.
  4. Bank Reconciliation 31 December: enter the balance per statement of account for every money journal and attach the statement.
  5. Run Checks runs all checks (account assignment, computation, asset register, statement); blockers and warnings are listed on the statement. Set the global lock date of the year before the finalisation.
  6. Finalise (accounting managers) runs all checks again and finalises the statement only without blockers, with all warnings acknowledged, with the questionnaire confirmed, the bank reconciled at 31 December, a lock date that covers 31 December, released form years and a computation that matches the current bookings. The statement, its values, slices, profit corrections, answers and bank reconciliation, the asset years of the year and the form years it uses can then no longer be changed or deleted, also not by an administrator. The data of the statement is stored as canonical JSON (amounts in cent) with its SHA-256, chained to the previous finalisation of the business. Keep finalised statements with their receipts for at least 10 years (§ 147 (3) AO).
  7. Check Integrity computes the hashes again, follows the chain and compares the finalised records and the content of their receipts with the stored data; the result is logged in the chatter. Changes made directly in the database cannot be prevented, but they become visible. Keep the payload SHA-256 and the chain hash of each finalisation also outside the database (e.g. the PDF of the fill-in aid): a consistent change of the latest finalisation with its hashes is visible only against them.
  8. Compare with Bookings computes a finalised year again into a comparison run next to the finalised one (not while a correction of the year is in progress: compute the correction instead). Every deviation per field, per sum of slices and of the bank balance at 31 December is the warning C-60; the finalised statement does not change. Every other computation of the year shows the deviations as well.
  9. Create Correction (accounting managers) creates a new version with a reason. It starts with the values, overrides, profit corrections and the bank reconciliation of the finalised version; the questionnaire is confirmed again. The tab Differences to the Previous Version lists what changed. When the correction is finalised, the previous version is superseded and stays readable.
  10. Enter the date and the transfer ticket of the submission on the tab Finalisation; these two fields can be entered after the finalisation.

Drilldown to the receipt. From a value of the statement, Show records lists its slices (group them by account, partner or month). A slice opens its money entry, its document, the attached receipts and the partial reconciliations the money passed; a value of the asset register opens the assets with their yearly values and journal items (Show assets); a value of a profit correction opens the correction and its own receipt. Backwards, the smart button EÜR Slices of a journal entry lists its slices with year and Kz.

Outputs (tab Outputs of a computed statement, also in the Print menu). All outputs show the values of the statement and only the data of its business; auditors (read-only accounting) can print and download them.

  • Fill-In Aid Mein ELSTER (HTML, PDF): the fields with a value and the mandatory header fields in the order of the official form, with line, Kz, official wording, value and previous year; sums are control values to compare with Mein ELSTER and to enter where Mein ELSTER does not compute them itself (not confirmed); Anlage AVEÜR per group and per asset; state, hash and versions; Kz 15.120 only after the questionnaire is confirmed. Enter the values in Mein ELSTER yourself: the module transmits nothing, and the fill-in aid is no form for a paper filing. The HTML view is accessible (tables with caption and header cells, the minus as text).
  • Evidence per Kennzahl (PDF, XLSX): every slice of every field with cash date, year, document, money entry, partner, account, tax, share, amount and source; the sum per field is the value of the statement.
  • Workbook (XLSX): EÜR, AVEÜR, evidence, asset register, low-value assets, asset pools, withdrawals and contributions, gifts per recipient (give-aways separately), business entertainment, check protocol and profit corrections.
  • List of the Kennzahlen for ELSTER (CSV, UTF-8, semicolon): year, form, version, box, line, Kz, code, official wording, amount, ELSTER field id, XPath, verified, source; only fields with a value. The ELSTER field ids are marked as not verified until they are checked against the official documentation.
  • Data Set (JSON, schema l10n_de_euer/1): year, form versions, business, fields, AVEÜR per group and per asset, state and the hash of the finalisation. Amounts are texts with a decimal point.
  • Data Access Z3 (ZIP, § 147 (6) AO): slices, field values, account assignment and the registers as CSV with header line, semicolon, decimal comma and CRLF, an index.xml after the description standard for the data transfer and the description of the fields as JSON.
  • Check Protocol (PDF, XLSX): all checks with their state and acknowledgement.
  • Procedure Documentation (HTML, PDF): a template from the data of the module (data flow, calculation rules, account assignment, settings, roles, lock concept, versions); complete it with the organisation of your business.

Setup and takeover (Accounting → Configuration → EÜR, accounting managers):

  1. EÜR Setup: enable cash basis accounting, enter the header values of the form, create the VAT status period (with the turnover of the previous year for the small business thresholds after a takeover), enter the start of the activity of a business founded recently, for small businesses switch the default accounts and taxes to the accounts without VAT (8195/3200, SKR04 4185/5200), set the private share account of the purchase journals, mark the money journals of a private account and choose the treatment of business entertainment. The wizard recommends the fiscal year lock date but never sets a lock date.
  2. Takeover from a Previous System: Read Opening Entries lists the open receivables and payables of the opening entries (opening move or entries with an opening balance account). Enter the category, the VAT treatment and the VAT rate of each original invoice and Take Over; without them the payment of the item is blocked (C-35). The turnover of the previous year goes into the VAT status period with the year it is entered for; without an amount nothing is stored unless No Turnover in the Previous Year confirms 0 (C-32). Take Over Asset Register opens the CSV takeover of the asset register.

Each form field carries its scope (supported, manual input, not supported).

Supported form years: 2024, 2025 and 2026 (Anlage EÜR, AVEÜR, SZ), all still in state Draft until verified. Without the form year of the assessment year (e.g. 2027 before the BMF letter) the module computes provisionally with the previous form year and blocks the finalisation.

Known uncertainties:

  • The profit determination (“Ermittlung des Gewinns”) has no printed box; the module uses box G. The assignment in the ELSTER data set is not confirmed.
  • ELSTER field ids are not part of the public data yet; their publication is being clarified.
  • Anlage SZ is computed with all its formulas, but filled in only if the other interest on debt (Kz 25.234) exceeds 2,050 €. Then the finalisation is blocked (C-41) until the carry-forward values of earlier years are supported.
  • Anlage LuF is not delivered; its transfer lines in Anlage EÜR are not supported.
  • A direct debit of the tax office collected on the working day after a due date on a weekend (10 January 2026 and 2027): the module proposes the year of payment (§ 108 (3), § 224 (2) no. 3 AO, H 11 EStH); the opposite decision (Apply, previous year) is possible with a warning (C-26) that must be acknowledged. The legal question is open with the tax advisor.
  • Rule parameters cover the supported years; low-value assets and asset pools from acquisitions in 2010, declining balance from 2020. Earlier periods are added when needed: a takeover of a declining balance asset delivered before 2020 needs the year of the switch to linear depreciation (or the parameters of that time).

Account assignment: the defaults for SKR03 and SKR04 cover all template accounts. Ambiguous accounts (cases A01–A34, e.g. software subscriptions, DATEV collective accounts 4500/4670, reverse charge purchase accounts) have low confidence and must be confirmed when used. The DATEV small business revenue accounts 8192/4184 are not part of the Odoo chart templates; they get their default assignment when you create them. DATEV auxiliary accounts of the EÜR (e.g. 8580–8589, 1449/1609 or SKR04 4580–4589) are reported as not provided for. Corrections of the delivered categories, mappings and defaults reach existing databases only through migration scripts (the data is loaded once).

Fixed assets: a new asset is always depreciated in full (R 7.4 (3) EStR); only an asset taken over from another system may keep its memo value (warning C-63). The month of disposal counts for the depreciation by default (setting). The choice between low-value assets and an asset pool is derived from the assets of the year (C-66): in a pool year every movable asset above 250 € up to 1,000 € goes into the pool. The use withdrawal for private use and the § 15a UStG correction are proposals that you confirm; the VAT on a use withdrawal spreads a cost of at least 500 € over the § 15a period. The private use of vehicles is not proposed by the register: it is the private use value of Kz 106 (profit corrections). § 7g (5) special depreciation needs a business use of at least 90 % in the year of delivery and the following year and the confirmation of the profit limit; § 7b special depreciation is limited to 5 % of the cost per year (the limits per square metre of § 7b (3) are your responsibility). Locked years keep their values: the form values of a locked year come from its stored yearly values, and what they depend on can no longer change.

A finalised statement is protected against changes through the user interface and the ORM of Odoo, also for administrators; database administrators can still change the data with SQL. Check Integrity finds a changed record or receipt; a consistent change of the latest finalisation together with its hashes is visible only against the hashes kept outside the database (e.g. with the PDF of the fill-in aid). It does not prevent changes. Locked asset years stay locked also for a correction of the year: correct their effect with an override of the value and a reason.

Small business limit: the limits of § 19 UStG apply to the whole enterprise of the owner (§ 2 (1) sentence 2 UStG). The module knows the turnover of the Odoo business only: turnover of other businesses outside Odoo (e.g. a photovoltaic system feeding into the grid) is not added, and the start of the activity is the start of the entrepreneurial activity as a whole. An owner with another business checks both limits himself with the total turnover of all businesses; the warnings of the module cover the Odoo turnover only. The turnover of the previous year comes from the bookings; after a takeover from another system it is entered at the VAT status period for the first year in Odoo (warning C-32 while it is missing; an empty amount is no input, a turnover of 0 is confirmed explicitly). Books that start with an opening entry during the previous year hold only part of it: the turnover of the whole year is entered the same way. A status period holds this input for one year; a second year that needs it gets a status period of its own from 1 January. Without an opening entry the module takes the books as complete: books that start during the previous year without one (e.g. bank statements imported from July) need an opening entry on their first day, otherwise the module compares the turnover of the part of the year in Odoo. Until 2024 the turnover of the year of foundation is converted into a turnover of the full year (§ 19 (3) sentences 3 and 4 UStG in the version until 2024); the law from 2025 has no conversion. The total turnover counts free supplies (Kz 106/108) only when they are taxable, at their VAT base (enter it on the profit correction; the asset register proposes it from the input tax deducted), never at their value for income tax. Free supplies booked on accounts count under standard taxation only when booked with output VAT (the net amount is the base); booked by a small business they do not count (hint C-32): enter the VAT base of a taxable one on a profit correction and leave its value empty. Income in kind that is tax-free or not taxable (Kz 103) does not count, like booked revenue of Kz 103.

Profit corrections: the yearly maximums (daily home office allowance, yearly flat rate of a home office, flat rate of business expenses per group, commuting allowance without own vehicle) are shared by all corrections of a year, the earlier ones first.

Outputs: the data access package refers to the document type definition of the description standard (gdpdu-01-09-2004.dtd), which is not shipped: its source and licence are being clarified. Add it to the package if your auditor needs it. The fill-in aid shows sums as control values because it is not confirmed whether Mein ELSTER computes them itself.

Reference cases: tests/data/pt01.json (small business) and tests/data/rt01.json (standard taxation) are synthetic cases with expected values calculated by hand and the derivation of every field; the tests book them and compare every field of both years. The values of RT-01 are not yet confirmed by a tax advisor (confirmation is empty). Open for that confirmation: flat rates without payment from the business (meals, daily home office allowance, home office, commuting allowance) count as contribution in Kz 123; a refund of income of a previous year reduces the income of its kind (Kz 112, 140) instead of being an expense (Kz 183).

Development status: Alpha. The module becomes Beta after a tax advisor has confirmed the expected values of the reference case RT-01 and has checked the first real statement computed with the module against the receipts and the statements of account (RT-04). Until a form year is verified by a person who did not create its data, a statement on it cannot be finalised (C-72).

Planned: Anlage SZ with the carry-forward values of earlier years, investment deductions (§ 7g (1)–(4) EStG), the private use of vehicles with the 1 % method and the cost cap, reserves (box 27), the form year 2027, draft entries for the depreciation of the asset register, a bridge to the asset management of the OCA, Odoo 20 and the contribution to OCA/l10n-germany; optionally the transmission via ERiC as a separate module. A blocked case is released only together with its tests.
